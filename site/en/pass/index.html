Season 1 · until 31 December

«Storm over Gravewater»

A hundred tiers of rewards in two months: the free track for everyone, the premium with a pass.

100 tiers in two months

A season lasts two calendar months; then the rewards change. The whole card takes about 300 hours of play; an hour a day reaches about tier 28. The first tiers come quicker.

Season XP for the things that matter

  • Port contracts
  • Quests and the campaign
  • The daily orders
  • Captains beaten
  • Boardings won
  • Lairs and creature fights
  • Bosses and zone bosses
  • World events
  • Your island’s buildings
  • Treasures dug up

Nothing drips by the second at sea: each source has its own daily cap.

Season challenges

Three challenges a day and four a week: each gives a big chunk of XP.

  • Finish 6 commissions: quests, contracts or the campaign+150
  • Deliver 3 port contracts+150
  • Win 4 boardings+150
  • Finish 35 commissions this week+900
  • Win 25 boardings this week+900
  • Win 15 fights with creatures this week+900

Nothing is lost

The rewards you earned and did not take by the season’s end come by letter: silver in it, the rest on your account or when you next come aboard.
