Heroes of the sea

Captains

A captain is a hero of Heroes: primary and secondary skills, talents, artifacts and a school of orders.

The Corsair

Edric Vane

«Last Volley»

Former Crown gunnery lieutenant, cashiered after he refused to fire on a surrendered merchant. He kept his discipline and lost his flag.

Universal naval fighter. Rewards clean broadsides, positioning and ammo choice.

Broadside DisciplineA full broadside (every gun on that side loaded) reloads 15% faster and flies 10% tighter.
  • Double ShotYour next broadside within 12 s fires two balls from every gun: +12% damage at rank 1 … +30% at rank 5.
  • Hard OverThrow the helm over: +80% … +110% turn rate for 4 s at the cost of 10% speed. The broadside fired in it or 3 s after rakes her: +10% … +20% (a rake every 45 s).
  • Spotter's EyeA spotter in the tops for 10 s: spread −20% … −35%, damage +3% … +5%, range +15%; each broadside has 15% … 35% to wreck her rudder or a mast.
  • Last VolleyULTIMATE. For 8 s (12 s at rank 4) the gun crews work like demons: broadsides more by a share of the fight, damage +10%. At rank 4 they ignore the alpha limit.

The Smuggler

Mara Quill

«The Fog Ledger»

Keeps two ledgers: one for the Crown, one for the truth. Every Fog Broker in the Whispering Archipelago owes her a favour — or fears the page with their name.

Trade, stealth and contraband. Avoids fights, wins by never being where the enemy looks.

False BottomContraband uses 30% less hold volume and is never found by patrol inspections. −10% detection signature.
  • Smoke PotsTar pots over the side: a smoke bank for 8 … 10 s. Incoming fire −45% … −50%, NPCs lose their lock; her first broadside out of it is an ambush.
  • Dark RunningAll lanterns out for 60 s: NPCs notice you at 40% of the usual distance; −10% speed. Ambush: her first broadside out of the dark or the smoke +65% … +66%, often with a critical on her rudder or her powder (again after 50 s).
  • Bribe SignalHoist the right flag and pay (the price grows with your level): Crown and League patrols ignore you for 60 … 120 s; from rank 3 the pirates of the sea too.
  • Vanish into FogULTIMATE. A fog bank swallows your ship: invisible beyond 250 m, +25% speed for 20 s. Knife in the fog: her next two broadsides go through 30% … 60% of armour, +15% … +30%.

The Reaver

Hask Morrow

«Red Hook»

Lost his hand to a Crown boarding axe at fourteen and replaced it with the axe-head. Forty ships later, merchants strike their colours when they see his hook painted on the sail.

Aggressive close combat. Grapeshot, ramming, boarding. Wins with crew and nerve, not range.

Blood in the WaterAgainst crews below 50%: +20% boarding power. Kills during boarding raise your morale.
  • Grapeshot FrenzyFor 10 s grapeshot reloads twice as fast and kills 30% … 60% more crew; the first grape broadside that lands takes 5% … 12% of her boarding army besides.
  • Ramming SpeedEvery hand to the braces: +35% speed and double acceleration for 5 s. A ram strikes ×2.9 … ×4.3: a share of her hull by your way into her (5% at ⚓1 for a plain ram at full way, a broadside's share higher).
  • War CryYour crew roars: +20 morale. Enemy crews within 300 m lose 15 … 25 morale and, shaken, reload 12% … 25% slower for 8 … 12 s.
  • Red Hook BoardingULTIMATE. For 10 s you may board from double range regardless of speed or damage, with +30% … +45% power; from rank 3 the hooks take 10% … 15% of her army before the fight.

The Navigator

Tobias Wren

«The Stargazer»

Charted the Dead Man's Expanse alone in a cutter and came back with a map nobody believed. Half of it has since been proven true. He does not talk about the other half.

Speed, weather, currents and exploration. First to every discovery, impossible to catch.

Reading the WindSees wind forecasts and currents. No-go zone −8°, currents +25%. The weather gauge: upwind of her mark, damage and range +10% (+25% by rank 5).
  • Trim SailsPerfect trim for 12 s: +20% … +30% speed.
  • Current RiderRide the stream for 20 s: currents push 150% harder, +20% turn rate; enemies within 300 m fight her current and lose 10% … 20% way and 15% … 25% turn.
  • Star FixTake a star fix: reveal every island within 7 km, and for 20 s the weak angles of enemies within 1.5 km — her broadsides on them rake, +12% … +20%.
  • Storm ChaserULTIMATE. The wind follows you: for 20 s you always sail at the best angle, +30% speed, and the guns load faster on it. At rank 4 allies within 500 m sail her wind.

The Drowned Captain · Premium

Ilse Harrow

«The Drowned»

Went down with the Saint Verity in the Drowned Crown. Walked out of the surf at Wrecktide eleven years later, dripping, calm, and very hungry for something that is not food.

Deep-sea mysticism. Area control, slows and attrition. Every miracle costs the crew's nerve.

Drowned OnceOnce per 5 min, lethal damage leaves you between water and light for 12 s: incoming damage −50%, guns still firing. Mend her to 10% hull or she sinks. Crown ports charge 20% more. Dread as a weapon: every 25 Dread costs enemies within 400 m 5 morale each 15 s; below 30 morale they reload 3% … 4% slower. She enters every fight with 30 … 40 Dread.
  • Deep Call30 Dread. After 1 s drowned hands rise at the target point (60 m) for 6 s: −40% speed and −30% turn inside, and every ship caught is holed and leaks 0.8 of a broadside over those seconds.
  • Brine Mend25 Dread. The sea knits your planks: 0.8 … 1 broadside of hull back over 8 s, a leak sealed, the rudder mended. Crew morale −6, and 2% of the crew go into the water.
  • Undertow35 Dread. A 400 × 60 m race of current from you toward the target for 10 s (×1 … ×1.4 by rank): ships sailing with it gain speed, against it lose it, and a ship with no way on is dragged along.
  • Maw of the DeepULTIMATE (100 resolve + 50 Dread). The water boils for 3 s, then a maw 45 m wide opens: 15% of each ship's hull (through armour), a mast and two leaks; ships within 90 m are dragged toward it. Again after 300 s (210 s at rank 4).

The Black Admiral · Premium

Cassius Dray

«The Black Admiral»

Commanded the Crown's Black Squadron until he sold its route charts to the Confederacy for a single signature. He still signs his letters "Admiral". Nobody corrects him.

Fleet command. Buffs allies, marks targets, calls escorts and bombardments. Strongest with friends.

Chain of CommandAllied and escort ships within 500 m reload 8% faster (15% by rank 5). +5% boarding power.
  • Form LineSignal flags: you and allies within 500 m get −30% … −40% spread and −8% … −10% reload for 15 s.
  • Mark TargetMark the ship nearest your cursor: she takes +7% … +9% damage from everyone for 15 s; from rank 3 everyone is ranged in on her weak side.
  • Call EscortPay the hire (it grows with your level): an escort a level below yours — a brig, a frigate, from the 50th a ship of the line; from the 60th two — joins you for 3 minutes, her broadsides a tenth of yours.
  • Admiralty BarrageULTIMATE. After 3 s, 12 … 18 mortar shells fall within 90 m of the target point, each 0.8% of the hull it strikes.

A dark Pirate Gothic sea: a ship is an army, a captain is a hero, boarding is a battle.
