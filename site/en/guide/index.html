    Sea and sailing

    SailingTap the sea to set a course; the ship sails there on its own.2 stepsPortsA port repairs the ship, trades goods and hires crew.3 stepsThe wheel and the sailsDrag the wheel the way to sail; «+» and «−» set the sail; the arc shows how much canvas is set; on a keyboard, W/A/S/D.4 stepsA course by tapTap the open sea and the helmsman takes her there; the pill on top shows the way left and «stop». Any turn of the wheel takes the helm back.3 stepsThe chart, «Where am I» and the minimapThe minimap (top right) opens the chart. Its chips switch the layers; «Where am I» brings your ship back into view.3 stepsWind and currentsWith the wind behind, up to +45 % speed; into the wind, slower, and dead into it you barely move. Currents carry the ship on their own.3 stepsStorms and the jury sailIn a storm, never sail with full canvas: it tears. Three quarters is safe. With the sails gone and no sailcloth, raise the jury sail.4 stepsThe hold and overloadTrade goods over half the hold (three quarters on a trader's hull) — overload, −20 % speed. Over the brim — −35 %. Ship's stores do not count.4 stepsShip's storesProvisions feed the crew, sailcloth mends the sails, planks mend the hull. They are light, every harbour sells them, and they cannot be sold.4 stepsRepairsAt sea, out of a fight: «Repair» (R) — the crew mend with planks and sailcloth. In port the yard mends everything for silver.3 stepsThe crew and moraleMorale tends to 70. Hunger, curses and black storms lower it, rum raises it. High spirits load faster and board harder; low ones slow everything.3 stepsLeaving the gameIn port you leave at once. At sea press «Exit» in the «⋯» tray: 30 s, and any order cancels it. A dropped connection leaves the ship at sea for 2 minutes.3 stepsBuffs and debuffsThe icons by the portrait are everything working on you now, the bad ones first, with the time left as a ring. Tap one for its card.3 steps

    Battle

    The ring of actionsTap a ship: a ring of what you can do with her comes up. «Attack», «Board» within 100 m, «Talk»; ⓘ opens her card.5 steps«Attack», «Close in», «Stand off»«Attack» puts the helmsman on her: he keeps gun range and turns your broadside to her. «Close in» goes for boarding, «Stand off» back to the guns, «Hold off» gives the wheel back.4 stepsRound, chain and grapeRound shot breaks the hull, chain tears the sails (she cannot run), grape kills the crew (before boarding). Tap the shot button to change it.3 stepsRangesEvery gun reaches 100 m farther than its base: a long nine fires round shot to 305 m. Chain reaches less, grape least.3 stepsBoardingWithin 100 m (hull to hull) «Board» throws the grapples: 2–4 s, and the ships are hauled together. Then the battle on the hexes.3 stepsThe boarding battle: the basicsA turn-based fight on an 11×9 field, as in Heroes. Initiative sets the order; shots at up to 6 hexes do full damage; flank +15 %, rear +30 %; cover −25 %.4 stepsMorale, luck and the twenty roundsGood morale may give a stack another turn, bad morale may freeze it; luck doubles a blow. After round 20 the side with more of its strength left wins.3 stepsAuto-battle and the order book«Auto» lets the crew fight for you (one auto-battle scroll a battle); «Quick combat» is free against a clearly weaker ship. The book holds your orders.3 stepsA pirate strikes her coloursA beaten pirate, patrol or hunter may ask for mercy. «Take all and sink her» — her purse and hold; «Take her crew and let her go» — 30 % of her crew and purse.4 stepsBeasts and monstersOrcas, sharks, whales, narwhals and serpents bite the hull, never the crew. Only «Attack» — no grapples. Flense the carcass for oil, bone and teeth.3 steps

    Flags and the law

    The three flagsNeutral — quiet scouting with an 85 % free hold. City or guild — peace with the law, fights with pirates and monsters. Pirate — hardcore: rob anyone, and pay with skulls.5 stepsSkulls and how to wash themUnder the pirate flag every peaceful victim is a skull. With one, the flag is locked; with two, lawful ports are shut. Wash them by time out of a fight or buy a pardon in a pirate haven.4 steps«Boarding: off»A switch on the flag card: no one boards you and you board no one — guns only. Against captains it works only under the neutral flag.3 stepsThe privateer patentA licence to hunt your city's enemies without skulls: sold in the ports of the Crown, the League and the Harpoon, from level 10, for 7 days.3 stepsPvP points and the monthly rewardsSinking a captain is 10 points, boarding 14, a duel won 5. Each calendar month the top ten get silver, doubloons and titles; everyone with 50+ gets 3000 silver.3 stepsName coloursOther captains' names are blue, a skulled captain's are red, and under the pirate flag the level is hidden behind «?».3 steps

    Trade

    The market and pricesEach port has its own prices, and they change every 8 hours. ▲ and ▼ show the trend; luxuries swing the most. Buy cheap here, sell dear there.3 stepsThe port card and «Market Sense»Tap a port on the chart for its card: what it sells and buys, cheap or dear. The «Market Sense» talent shows the exact prices you remember.2 stepsHow much room goods takeEvery good says its room per unit, «m³/unit». Cheap goods are compact, valuable ones bulky: a unit of ambergris takes more than 80 bolts of sailcloth.3 stepsThe warehouseA warehouse of 120 m³ in any port: rent is paid once, on the first deposit. «Store all» and «Take all» for each good.3 stepsContracts and merchants' runs«Quests» → «Contracts»: the port's contracts and bounties, the merchants' runs (buy here, deliver there by the deadline) and the Admiralty's board.2 stepsThe exchange and the auctionIn bigger ports the «Exchange»: forwards, buy orders and the bank. In the Free Harbors the trophy auction: 10-minute lots, the house keeps a tenth.5 stepsDoubloons into silverIn the shop «Exchange» turns doubloons into silver at 1 : 200. One way only.2 steps

    Travel

    The passage between hubsSeven hub harbours are linked by the «Passage»: 800 to 15 000 silver by distance, a trip at ten times the speed, untouchable, docking on arrival.4 stepsThe passage's rulesOnce in 30 minutes. Under the pirate flag ×2, towards a quest's goal ×0.25. Not with skulls, not in a fight, and not with trade goods over 60 % of the hold.4 stepsThe quiet harbourWithin 1 km of every hub no captain can attack another. After a passage the protection follows you for 3 more minutes.2 steps«To my island»«To my island» in the «⋯» tray or at the bottom of the chart sets the course home; with no island yet, to the nearest free one.3 steps«Find foe»A list of the pirates, beasts, monsters and lairs you can see or have charted within 30 km, filtered by kind and level. Tap a row for the course.4 steps
