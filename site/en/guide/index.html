    Sea and sailing

    SailingTap the sea to set a course; the ship sails there on its own.2 stepsPortsA port repairs the ship, trades goods and hires crew.3 stepsThe wheel and the sailsDrag the wheel the way to sail; «+» and «−» set the sail; the arc shows how much canvas is set; on a keyboard, W/A/S/D.4 stepsA course by tapTap the open sea and the helmsman takes her there; the pill on top shows the way left and «stop». Any turn of the wheel takes the helm back.3 stepsThe chart, «Where am I» and the minimapThe minimap (top right) opens the chart. Its chips switch the layers; «Where am I» brings your ship back into view.3 stepsWind and currentsWith the wind behind, up to +45 % speed; into the wind, slower, and dead into it you barely move. Currents carry the ship on their own.3 stepsStorms and the jury sailIn a storm, never sail with full canvas: it tears. Three quarters is safe. With the sails gone and no sailcloth, raise the jury sail.4 stepsThe hold and overloadTrade goods over half the hold (three quarters on a trader's hull) — overload, −20 % speed. Over the brim — −35 %. Ship's stores do not count.4 stepsShip's storesProvisions feed the crew, sailcloth mends the sails, planks mend the hull. They are light, every harbour sells them, and they cannot be sold.4 stepsRepairsAt sea, out of a fight: «Repair» (R) — the crew mend with planks and sailcloth. In port the yard mends everything for silver.3 stepsThe crew and moraleMorale tends to 70. Hunger, curses and black storms lower it, rum raises it. High spirits load faster and board harder; low ones slow everything.3 stepsLeaving the gameIn port you leave at once. At sea press «Exit» in the «⋯» tray: 30 s, and any order cancels it. A dropped connection leaves the ship at sea for 2 minutes.3 stepsBuffs and debuffsThe icons by the portrait are everything working on you now, the bad ones first, with the time left as a ring. Tap one for its card.3 steps

    Battle

    The ring of actionsTap a ship: a ring of what you can do with her comes up. «Attack», «Board» within 100 m, «Talk»; ⓘ opens her card.5 steps«Attack», «Close in», «Stand off»«Attack» puts the helmsman on her: he keeps gun range and turns your broadside to her. «Close in» goes for boarding, «Stand off» back to the guns, «Hold off» gives the wheel back.4 stepsRound, chain and grapeRound shot breaks the hull, chain tears the sails (she cannot run), grape kills the crew (before boarding). Tap the shot button to change it.3 stepsRangesEvery gun reaches 100 m farther than its base: a long nine fires round shot to 305 m. Chain reaches less, grape least.3 stepsBoardingWithin 100 m (hull to hull) «Board» throws the grapples: 2–4 s, and the ships are hauled together. Then the battle on the hexes.3 stepsThe boarding battle: the basicsA turn-based fight on an 11×9 field, as in Heroes. Initiative sets the order; shots at up to 6 hexes do full damage; flank +15 %, rear +30 %; cover −25 %.4 stepsMorale, luck and the twenty roundsGood morale may give a stack another turn, bad morale may freeze it; luck doubles a blow. After round 20 the side with more of its strength left wins.3 stepsAuto-battle and the order book«Auto» lets the crew fight for you (one auto-battle scroll a battle); «Quick combat» is free against a clearly weaker ship. The book holds your orders.3 stepsA pirate strikes her coloursA beaten pirate, patrol or hunter may ask for mercy. «Take all and sink her» — her purse and hold; «Take her crew and let her go» — 30 % of her crew and purse.4 stepsBeasts and monstersOrcas, sharks, whales, narwhals and serpents bite the hull, never the crew. Only «Attack» — no grapples. Flense the carcass for oil, bone and teeth.3 steps

    Flags and the law

    The three flagsNeutral — quiet scouting with an 85 % free hold. City or guild — peace with the law, fights with pirates and monsters. Pirate — hardcore: rob anyone, and pay with skulls.5 stepsSkulls and how to wash themUnder the pirate flag every peaceful victim is a skull. With one, the flag is locked; with two, lawful ports are shut. Wash them by time out of a fight or buy a pardon in a pirate haven.4 steps«Boarding: off»A switch on the flag card: no one boards you and you board no one — guns only. Against captains it works only under the neutral flag.3 stepsThe privateer patentA licence to hunt your city's enemies without skulls: sold in the ports of the Crown, the League and the Harpoon, from level 10, for 7 days.3 stepsPvP points and the monthly rewardsSinking a captain is 10 points, boarding 14, a duel won 5. Each calendar month the top ten get silver, doubloons and titles; everyone with 50+ gets 3000 silver.3 stepsName coloursOther captains' names are blue, a skulled captain's are red, and under the pirate flag the level is hidden behind «?».3 steps

    Trade

    The market and pricesEach port has its own prices, and they change every 8 hours. ▲ and ▼ show the trend; luxuries swing the most. Buy cheap here, sell dear there.3 stepsThe port card and «Market Sense»Tap a port on the chart for its card: what it sells and buys, cheap or dear. The «Market Sense» talent shows the exact prices you remember.2 stepsHow much room goods takeEvery good says its room per unit, «m³/unit». Cheap goods are compact, valuable ones bulky: a unit of ambergris takes more than 80 bolts of sailcloth.3 stepsThe warehouseA warehouse of 120 m³ in any port: rent is paid once, on the first deposit. «Store all» and «Take all» for each good.3 stepsContracts and merchants' runs«Quests» → «Contracts»: the port's contracts and bounties, the merchants' runs (buy here, deliver there by the deadline) and the Admiralty's board.2 stepsThe exchange and the auctionIn bigger ports the «Exchange»: forwards, buy orders and the bank. In the Free Harbors the trophy auction: 10-minute lots, the house keeps a tenth.5 stepsDoubloons into silverIn the shop «Exchange» turns doubloons into silver at 1 : 200. One way only.2 steps

    The island

    Claiming an islandAny island without a port can be yours: the first one is free and immediate.5 stepsFirst steps on the islandThree guided steps with rewards: the Marine Barracks, hiring marines, the Shooting Range.5 stepsThe town and its dwellingsThe Town Hall sets the island's level; seven dwellings train your army tier by tier.5 stepsBuilding costs and the treasuryLevels 1 and 2 are almost free; the treasury pays first, then your purse.4 steps«Bring in»: silver and goods to the islandSilver goes into the treasury from anywhere; goods are unloaded off the island; the barge brings timber, coal and iron.5 stepsDemolishing a buildingA building can be razed for half its cost back; the plot is freed.3 stepsForge, workshop and anvilGear is tempered in the Forge (Town Hall 3+); artifacts are made in the Workshop and reforged on the Anvil (a Market is needed).4 stepsProducers and the yardLumber camps, quarries and mines fill the yard by the hour; collect with «Collect».4 stepsIsland boatsFar from home, load men from your island's dwellings into boats: they catch you up anywhere at sea.5 steps«Where to get it»Every cost chip tells where to get what is missing and can sail you to the nearest seller.3 stepsRushing a buildFree 15-minute speed-ups come every day; the rest is finished for doubloons.4 steps

    The army

    Hiring an armyIn port: «Army» → «Recruit an army». Pick a kind, set the number, «Hire».5 stepsIsland troops in any portEvery dwelling of tier 2–7 on your island opens that tier for hire in every port.4 stepsStacks: split, merge, dismissIn «The Company» (key O) or «Recruit»: tap a stack, then «Split» or «Dismiss»; drop it on its kind to merge.5 steps«Might X/Y»: the ship's limitThe ship's level caps how strong an army it carries; deckhands are never capped.3 stepsTiers and upgradesSeven tiers, each with an upgraded kind; an upgraded dwelling retrains men for the price difference.3 stepsGift stacksA stack marked «gift» fights like any other, but its losses are never refilled.3 stepsThe surgeon and resurrectionMen fallen in the last 24 hours come back for silver at the surgeon in any port.3 stepsBerths: keeping several ships3 berths are free besides the ship you sail; each more costs 300 doubloons, up to 10. Swapping takes 2 minutes.5 stepsConvoy and hired guardsConvoy ships are hired at the harbour master once you command; merchants at sea can be hired as guards for a while.3 steps

    Exploring

    Treasure mapsA map draws a search circle on the chart; inside it «Warm → Warmer → Hot!» leads you to a scroll on the water — dig there.6 stepsWrecks and diversOver a sunken wreck press «Divers»: in 25 s they bring up its cargo; the depth decides if they reach it.4 stepsLanding partiesAt a ruin, a cache or a mine, «Land» sends a party ashore; it comes back with the haul.3 stepsWork at a spot: the countdownDivers, a landing, a dig: a countdown at the top, the ship at anchor; «Call off» brings the men back.3 stepsMines and your flagBeat a mine's guards, land and raise your flag: it pays every sea day. Up to 7 mines.5 stepsLairs and their returnA beaten lair comes back in 2–4 sea days; its full loot is yours once a week.3 stepsSea marksDriftwood, wreck fields, fairway buoys, lanterns, floating bones, ice floes: stop by one and press its button.3 stepsLookouts and lighthousesClimb a lookout to chart 5 km of sea; a lit lighthouse shows the shoals at night.3 stepsThe chart of the known seaKey M. Layers pick what is drawn; «Key» explains every mark; buttons find you, your island and a foe.4 steps«Chronicle»: everything that happened«Journal» → «Chronicle»: the last 300 events with filters.3 stepsThe fog of warThe sea is dark until you see it; once seen, it stays on your chart.3 stepsThe navigator's distance perksEvery 100 km sailed is a page of the log: «Cartographer» pays experience, «Pathfinder» morale and speed.3 steps

    Travel

    The passage between hubsSeven hub harbours are linked by the «Passage»: 800 to 15 000 silver by distance, a trip at ten times the speed, untouchable, docking on arrival.4 stepsThe passage's rulesOnce in 30 minutes. Under the pirate flag ×2, towards a quest's goal ×0.25. Not with skulls, not in a fight, and not with trade goods over 60 % of the hold.4 stepsThe quiet harbourWithin 1 km of every hub no captain can attack another. After a passage the protection follows you for 3 more minutes.2 steps«To my island»«To my island» in the «⋯» tray or at the bottom of the chart sets the course home; with no island yet, to the nearest free one.3 steps«Find foe»A list of the pirates, beasts, monsters and lairs you can see or have charted within 30 km, filtered by kind and level. Tap a row for the course.4 steps

    Growing stronger

    Levels and experienceUp to level 60. Quests are the steadiest source of experience; each level gives a talent point.3 stepsTalents: the wind roseTen trees around the wind rose; tiers open at 0/5/10/15/20 points in a tree.3 stepsSkills of the captainHoMM-style: primary skills grow every level, secondary skills are picked one of two; sea abilities rank up with the level.3 stepsThe campaign36 chapters, 720 quests: one at a time, the next comes by itself when your level allows.3 stepsQuests, grouped by the giverTake quests from a port's board; the journal groups them by the place to hand them in, nearest first.3 stepsContracts and daily ordersContracts pay silver and experience; three daily orders and their chest reset at midnight UTC.3 stepsThe season card100 tiers in two months; a free track and a premium one. Season experience comes from almost everything you do.5 stepsThe rating: «Level» and «Might»«Might» is 35 % army, 25 % ship, 20 % island and 20 % gear; the site's rating has boards for each.3 stepsThe PvP boardPoints for captains sunk and boarded; the month's top ten get titles, doubloons and silver.3 stepsPromo codes«Settings» → «Promo code» → «Enter»: type the code and press «Activate».3 stepsThe daily giftA 28-day track, one claim a day; one missed day is forgiven.3 stepsThe doubloon shopDoubloons buy speed-ups, supplies, scrolls, ship looks and island help; «Top up» buys doubloons.3 steps

    Questions and answers

    Where do I buy a bigger ship?Bigger hulls are sold only at higher-rank shipyards: the chart's «Shipyards» layer shows each yard's rank and ship levels.3 stepsHow do I bring silver and goods to my island?Island → «Bring in»: silver into the treasury from anywhere, goods unloaded off the island, or a barge for silver.3 stepsHow do I take a mine?Beat its guards, land by it and raise your flag in 20 seconds. Up to 7 mines.3 stepsWhere do I temper gear and forge artifacts?Gear: the island's «Forge» (Town Hall 3+) or a port with a shipyard of rank 2+. Artifacts: the «Workshop» and the «Anvil» (a Market is needed).3 stepsHow many ships can I keep?Three berths free plus the ship under you — four in all; more for 300 doubloons each, up to ten.3 stepsWhen does a lair come back?A shore lair in 1 h 36 min, a grotto in 2 h 24 min, a guardian in 3 h 12 min; full loot once a sea week.3 stepsWhy can't I hire marines?Your island's marines are hired only once the Marine Barracks stand; big ports have marines of their own.3 stepsHow does the «Passage» between ports work?Seven hub ports; open the «Passage» tab, pick an opened hub, pay. 800 to 15 000 silver, 30 minutes' cooldown.3 stepsWhat does my crew do while I am away?At sea they eat provisions, in port they drink silver — a quarter of the usual, for at most a day.3 stepsCan I just close the tab at sea?No escape that way: at sea the ship stays 2 minutes and can be attacked. Use «Exit»: 30 seconds.3 stepsWhere is my gift letter?«Journal» → «Letters», in any port: «Receive». No room aboard — dismiss someone, the letter waits.3 stepsWhy does the surgeon offer to raise deckhands?The surgeon raises exactly who fell in the last 24 hours — by kind, at the price of their kind.3 steps
